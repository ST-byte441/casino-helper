# Casino Helper Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 14, 2026

Casino Helper ("the app") is built by ST-byte441. It's a practice and learning app for casino table games (Blackjack, Craps, Baccarat and Roulette). It uses play money only: there is no real-money gambling, no wagering and no purchases.

## Summary

The app does **not** collect, sell or share your personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you create in the app stays on your device.

## Information stored on your device

The app saves the following **locally on your device only**. It is never uploaded to us or anyone else:

- **Player profiles:** names you enter, play-money bankroll and game statistics (for example hands played, win rate and net result).
- **Table settings:** your rule and preference choices for each game.
- **Profile pictures:** if you choose your own photo, the app copies it into its private storage on your device and shows it as your profile picture.

You can delete this data at any time by deleting a profile in the app, clearing the app's storage in your device settings, or uninstalling the app.

## Permissions

- **Photos / media:** used only when you choose a photo for your profile picture. The app reads only the photo you pick. It does not use the camera.

## Network connections

The app makes a few network requests to keep itself up to date. None of them include personal information you entered in the app.

- **App updates (Expo):** on launch, the app checks Expo's update service (expo.dev) for new versions of its game content and code. Like any web request, this exposes your device's IP address and basic technical details (such as app version and platform) to Expo. See [Expo's privacy policy](https://expo.dev/privacy).
- **Release check (GitHub), only for copies installed outside Google Play:** the app checks GitHub for a newer version to download. This exposes your IP address to GitHub. See [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Copies installed from Google Play skip this check.

If you install the app from Google Play, Google may collect information as described in [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Age requirement

The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at anyone under 18, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
