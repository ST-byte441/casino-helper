## The Wheel

- The wheel has **36 numbers, 1 to 36, plus a "0" and/or a "00"**. *(NGCB)*
- **0 and 00 are green.** *(NGCB, GN, Wiki)*
- Every number from 1 to 36 is either red or black: *(GN, Wiki)*

| Colour | Numbers |
|---|---|
| **Red** (18) | 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 12, 14, 16, 18, 19, 21, 23, 25, 27, 30, 32, 34, 36 |
| **Black** (18) | 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 11, 13, 15, 17, 20, 22, 24, 26, 28, 29, 31, 33, 35 |
| **Green** | 0, 00 (and 000 on triple-zero wheels) |

- **Colour rule:** in 1–10 and 19–28, odd numbers are red and even numbers are black. In 11–18 and 29–36, odd numbers are black and even numbers are red. *(Wiki)* It matches the GN list exactly.
- **Colour is not the same as odd/even.** Each colour has 9 odd and 9 even numbers, so Red/Black and Odd/Even are separate bets. **(derived)**
- **Around the wheel, the colours alternate red and black**, apart from the green zeros. *(NGCB says "alternately coloured". This was checked against all three wheel sequences below.)*

### Pocket order (clockwise, "in most casinos") *(Wiki)*

| Wheel | Pockets | Sequence |
|---|---|---|
| **Double zero** (American) | 38 | 0-28-9-26-30-11-7-20-32-17-5-22-34-15-3-24-36-13-1-00-27-10-25-29-12-8-19-31-18-6-21-33-16-4-23-35-14-2 |
| **Single zero** (European/French) | 37 | 0-32-15-19-4-21-2-25-17-34-6-27-13-36-11-30-8-23-10-5-24-16-33-1-20-14-31-9-22-18-29-7-28-12-35-3-26 |
| **Triple zero** | 39 | 0-000-00-32-15-19-4-21-2-25-17-34-6-27-13-36-11-30-8-23-10-5-24-16-33-1-20-14-31-9-22-18-29-7-28-12-35-3-26 |

The pocket order is only needed for wheel animation and for the European call bets. It has no effect on bet results.

## Table Layout

- The numbers 1–36 sit in **3 columns × 12 rows**. Row *r* holds `3r−2, 3r−1, 3r`, so the rows are 1-2-3, 4-5-6 … 34-35-36. *(GN and Wiki column lists; Wiki street examples)*
- The zeros sit **at the head of the layout, above the 1-2-3 row**. On a double-zero layout, **0 is next to 1** and **00 is next to 3**. *(Wiki, implied by Top Line chip placement "on the outer corner shared by 0–1 or 00–3")*
- The layout is either single-zero or double-zero. *(Wiki)*
- **Column bets** are placed on the space below the last number in the column (34, 35 or 36). *(Wiki)*

| Column | Numbers |
|---|---|
| Column 1 | 1, 4, 7, 10, 13, 16, 19, 22, 25, 28, 31, 34 |
| Column 2 | 2, 5, 8, 11, 14, 17, 20, 23, 26, 29, 32, 35 |
| Column 3 | 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18, 21, 24, 27, 30, 33, 36 |

*(Wiki, GN. GN's Column 3 is missing the number 3; see Errata.)*

## Variants

### American (double zero)
- 36 numbers plus **0 and 00**: two green squares. *(Venetian, NGCB, GN, Wiki)*
- The Venetian describes this as its most popular version. *(Venetian)*
- Most casinos use the American-style table with the wheel at one end. *(Wiki)*
- **This is the default variant for this file.** All odds below are for 38 pockets. *(GN)*

### European (single zero)
- 36 numbers plus a **single 0**: one green square. *(Venetian, GN, Wiki)*
- Has a lower house edge than American (see House Edge). *(Wiki)*
- The Venetian describes it as a favourite among international players. *(Venetian)*
- The **French-style** table has the wheel in the centre and a layout on each side. Wikipedia says it is rarely found outside Monte Carlo. *(Wiki)*

### Triple zero
- Has **0, 00 and 000**: 39 pockets. *(Wiki)*
- **Venetian Roulette** is a triple-zero layout. Its third zero space is shown as the Venetian logo or a **"V"**. *(Venetian)*

### Roulette Xtra / Lightning Roulette
- After **"No more bets"** is called, **4 or 5 numbers** are chosen at random to get boosted **straight-up** payouts, from **30 to 1 up to 200 to 1**. *(Venetian)*

## How a Round Is Played

1. **Place bets.** Players place one or more wagers on the numbered layout. *(NGCB)* They may make as many bets as they like. *(GN)*
2. **Spin.** The dealer spins the wheel one way and the ball the **opposite way**. *(NGCB, Wiki)*
3. **No more bets.** Bets may be placed while the ball is spinning, until the dealer calls **"No more bets"** (French: *"rien ne va plus"*). *(NGCB, Wiki)*
4. **Result.** The ball makes **two to three rotations** *(NGCB)*, loses momentum, passes through the deflectors *(Wiki)* and must **land in a numbered pocket** to decide the outcome. *(NGCB)*
5. **Mark.** The dealer places a **marker (the "dolly")** on the winning number. *(NGCB, Wiki)* While the dolly is on the table, **no one may place, collect or remove bets**. *(Wiki)*
6. **Settle.** The dealer sweeps away all losing bets, then pays the winning inside and outside bets. *(Wiki)*
7. **Clear.** The dealer removes the dolly. **Winning chips stay on the layout until the player picks them up**, so an untouched winning bet rides on the next spin. The stake is returned with the payout. *(Wiki)*

- **Each spin is independent.** No number is ever "due". *(GN; Wiki: gambler's fallacy)*

## Bets and Payouts

Payouts are shown as "X to 1": the player keeps the stake and is paid X times it. Win chances are for an **American wheel (38 pockets)**. *(GN)*

**Payout formula:** a bet covering *k* numbers pays `36/k − 1` to 1. *(Wiki: "the payout is 36/p")* For example, 12 numbers pay 36/12 − 1 = 2 to 1. Every standard bet follows this formula except Top Line (see below).

### Inside bets

| Bet | Covers | Chip placement *(Wiki)* | Payout | Win chance (38) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Straight Up** | 1 number (including 0 or 00) | Inside the number's square | **35 to 1** | 1/38 (2.63%) | NGCB, Venetian, GN, Wiki |
| **Split** | 2 numbers next to each other, vertically or horizontally (e.g. 14–17, 8–9) | On the shared edge | **17 to 1** | 2/38 (5.26%) | NGCB, Venetian, GN, Wiki |
| **Courtesy Line / Row** | 0 and 00 | Between 0 and 00 | **17 to 1** | 2/38 (5.26%) | NGCB, Wiki |
| **Street / Across** | 3 numbers in a row (1–2–3 … 34–35–36) | Outer edge of the row | **11 to 1** | 3/38 (7.89%) | NGCB, Venetian, GN, Wiki |
| **Trio / Basket** | 0–1–2 or 0–00–2 or 00–2–3 (double zero); 0–1–2 or 0–2–3 (single zero) | Corner shared by the three numbers | **11 to 1** (derived from the formula) | 3/38 (7.89%) | Wiki |
| **Corner / Square** | 4 numbers meeting at a corner (e.g. 10–11–13–14) | On the shared corner | **8 to 1** | 4/38 (10.53%) | NGCB, Venetian, GN, Wiki |
| **First Five / Top Line** (double zero only) | 0, 00, 1, 2, 3 | Outer corner shared by 0–1 or 00–3 | **6 to 1** | 5/38 (13.16%) | NGCB, Venetian, GN, Wiki |
| **First Four** (single zero only) | 0, 1, 2, 3 | Outer corner shared by 0–1 or 0–3 | **8 to 1** | 4/37 | Wiki |
| **Six Line / Double Street / Across** | 2 adjacent rows of 3 (1–6, 4–9 … 31–36) | Outer corner shared by the two rows | **5 to 1** | 6/38 (15.79%) | NGCB, Venetian, GN, Wiki |

- **First Five / Top Line** is the only 5-number bet *(Venetian)*, and it is **the worst bet on the double-zero table**. The correct payout would be 6⅕ to 1, but it pays 6 to 1, giving a **7.89% house edge** instead of 5.26%. *(Wiki)* This is worth flagging in the helper.

### Outside bets

All outside bets **lose if a zero comes up**, unless la partage or en prison applies (see Zero Rules). *(Wiki)*

| Bet | Covers | Payout | Win chance (38) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Dozen** | 1st 12 (1–12, *P12*), 2nd 12 (13–24, *M12*), 3rd 12 (25–36, *D12*) | **2 to 1** | 12/38 (31.58%) | NGCB, Venetian, GN, Wiki |
| **Column** | 12 numbers in a column (see Table Layout) | **2 to 1** | 12/38 (31.58%) | NGCB, Venetian, GN, Wiki |
| **Snake** (some casinos only) | Red numbers 1, 5, 9, 12, 14, 16, 19, 23, 27, 30, 32, 34 | **2 to 1** | 12/38 (31.58%) | Wiki |
| **Low** (1 to 18, *manque*) | The first 18 numbers | **1 to 1** | 18/38 (47.37%) | NGCB, Venetian, GN, Wiki |
| **High** (19 to 36, *passe*) | The second 18 numbers | **1 to 1** | 18/38 (47.37%) | NGCB, Venetian, GN, Wiki |
| **Odd** (*impair*) | 18 odd numbers | **1 to 1** | 18/38 (47.37%) | NGCB, Venetian, GN, Wiki |
| **Even** (*pair*) | 18 even numbers | **1 to 1** | 18/38 (47.37%) | NGCB, Venetian, GN, Wiki |
| **Red** (*rouge*) / **Black** (*noir*) | 18 red or 18 black numbers | **1 to 1** | 18/38 (47.37%) | NGCB, Venetian, GN, Wiki |

- **0 is neither even nor odd** in roulette. *(Wiki)* None of the zeros is red or black, low or high, or in any dozen or column. *(Wiki: "all of these bets lose if a 0 comes up")*
- The snake bet's chip goes on the lower corner of the 34 square, where it meets the 19–36 box. *(Wiki)*

## Zero Rules for Even-Money Bets *(Wiki)*

These are optional house rules that apply only to the **even-money outside bets** (Low/High, Red/Black, Odd/Even) when a zero comes up:

- **La partage ("halfback"):** the player loses only **half** the bet.
- **En prison:** the bet is "imprisoned" rather than lost. If the next spin would win for that bet, the wager is **returned**.
- With en prison, the house edge on these bets drops to **1.35–1.38% on a single-zero wheel** and **2.63% on a double-zero wheel**.
- No source gives the exact house edge under la partage.

## House Edge *(Wiki unless noted)*

| Wheel | Pockets | House edge (standard bets) |
|---|---|---|
| Single zero | 37 | **2.70%** |
| Double zero | 38 | **5.26%** |
| Double zero, First Five / Top Line bet | 38 | **7.89%** |
| Triple zero | 39 | **7.69%** |

- **Formula:** the expected value of a $1 bet is `36/n − 1`, where *n* is the number of pockets. With 36 pockets the game would break even. "Having 37 or more numbers gives the casino its edge."
- The zeros create the house edge, but it is small compared with other casino games. *(GN)*
- **Bets with the same win chance are mathematically the same.** For example, Red, Even and High are equivalent over time. *(GN)* Every standard bet has the same house edge on a given wheel. Only the risk (variance) differs. *(Wiki: same expected value for every bet except Top Line)*
- The more numbers a bet covers, the more likely it is to win and the less it pays. *(GN)*

## Announced Bets: Sections of the Wheel *(Wiki; single-zero wheel only)*

These "French bets" cover sections of the wheel rather than of the layout. They are called by the player, who puts up the chips immediately. Not every casino offers them. Each is a bundle of ordinary inside bets, so each chip pays at its normal inside-bet rate.

| Bet | Numbers (in wheel order) | Chips | Chip placement |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Voisins du zéro** (neighbours of zero) | 17 numbers: 22-18-29-7-28-12-35-3-26-0-32-15-19-4-21-2-25 | 9 | 2 on the 0-2-3 trio; 1 each on splits 4–7, 12–15, 18–21, 19–22, 32–35; 2 on the 25-26-28-29 corner |
| **Jeu zéro** (zero game) | 7 numbers: 12-35-3-26-0-32-15 | 4 | 1 each on splits 0–3, 12–15, 32–35; 1 straight up on 26 |
| **Tiers du cylindre** (third of the wheel) | 12 numbers: 27-13-36-11-30-8-23-10-5-24-16-33 | 6 | 1 each on splits 5–8, 10–11, 13–16, 23–24, 27–30, 33–36 |
| **Orphelins** (orphans) | 8 numbers: 17-34-6 and 1-20-14-31-9 | 5 | 1 straight up on 1; 1 each on splits 6–9, 14–17, 17–20, 31–34 |
| **… and the neighbours** | A number plus the 2 on each side of it on the wheel | 5 | 1 straight up on each of the 5 numbers (e.g. "0 and the neighbours" = 3, 26, 0, 32, 15) |
| **Final *n*** | Every number ending in digit *n* | 4 for finals 0–6, 3 for finals 7–9 | 1 straight up on each (e.g. Final 4 = 4, 14, 24, 34) |

- Voisins, Tiers and Orphelins together cover all 37 pockets with no overlap. **(derived; checked against the wheel sequence)**

## Table Limits and Maximum Bets *(Wiki)*

- Tables set **minimum and maximum bets**, usually applied **separately** to a player's inside bets and outside bets on each spin. No source gives specific amounts.
- In European roulette the maximum often scales with the numbers covered. If the straight-up maximum is $1,000, the maximums are **$2,000 per split, $3,000 per street, $4,000 per corner and $6,000 per six line**.
- A **"full complete"** (maximum) bet on a number covers every inside bet that includes it. For example, "17 to the maximum" is 40 chips and pays 392 chips when 17 hits.

## Chips and Table Conduct

- **Roulette chips:** each player uses a **different colour** of chip, so bets are not confused. A chip's value is set by the price paid for it. *(Venetian, Wiki)*
- **Cash out at the same table.** Roulette chips must be redeemed at the table where they were bought. Away from that table, nobody knows what they are worth. *(Venetian)*
- **Placement is the bet.** Where a chip sits decides the wager. Each player is responsible for placing their wager correctly, whether the player or the dealer puts the chip down. *(Venetian)*

## Betting Systems (for the helper)

- **No betting system can change the odds in the player's favour.** "The more bets a player makes, the more they are going to lose", whatever strategy they use. *(Wiki)*
- Systems rely on the **gambler's fallacy**: the idea that past spins affect future ones. *(Wiki, GN)*
- **Combining bets** spreads risk and flattens rewards. It does not change the house edge. *(GN, Wiki)*

| System | Type | How it works *(Wiki)* | Flaw *(Wiki)* |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Martingale** | Negative progression | Double the bet after each loss, so the first win recovers all losses plus one unit | A long losing streak or the table maximum wipes out the bankroll |
| **Reverse Martingale (Paroli)** | Positive progression | Double the bet after each win | Same problem as Martingale: one loss gives back everything |
| **Labouchère** | Negative progression | Write a line of numbers; bet first + last. On a win, cross them out. On a loss, add the bet to the end | Bet sizes grow quickly during losing runs |
| **D'Alembert** | Negative progression | Add one unit after a loss; remove one unit after a win | Relies on the gambler's fallacy |
| **Fibonacci** | Negative progression | Named only as an example of a negative progression | — |

- **Negative progression** systems give small, steady wins with occasional catastrophic losses. **Positive progression** systems give small, steady losses with occasional big wins. Both lose on average. *(Wiki)*

## Source Errata

The GN page has two mistakes. This file uses the corrected versions:

1. **Column 3 is missing the number 3.** GN lists "6, 9, 12 … 36", which is only 11 numbers. The correct list is 3, 6, 9 … 36, confirmed by Wiki's column table, the 12/38 win chance and the NGCB definition ("12 chosen numbers in a column").
2. **High and Low are swapped.** GN says "High - 1-18, Low - 19-36". The correct split is **Low = 1–18** and **High = 19–36** (NGCB: "the first 18 numbers" and "the second 18 numbers"; Wiki: 1–18 low/*manque*, 19–36 high/*passe*).
